JobHunter AI

Built an AI job-hunting assistant after getting tired of rewriting resumes for every application. Runs entirely offline using Ollama and Llama 3.2, tailoring resumes from a single master profile, generating ATS-friendly DOCX files, and highlighting missing skills without inventing experience. Designed as the foundation for a fully autonomous job application agent. Built with Python, Ollama, JSON, and DOCX generation.
