Fashion Retail Demand Forecasting

My MSc thesis - can a small retailer predict what'll sell next month using sales data, Google Trends, and (weirdly) Reddit? Turns out: kind of, yes. XGBoost did most of the heavy lifting, landed around 74% accuracy. Wrapped it in a Streamlit app so humans could actually use it.

Smart Accident Severity Detection
